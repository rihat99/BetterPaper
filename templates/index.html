Patterns: the frozen encoder carries most of the gain, and the code often differs from the paper

Each point combines the critical analyses of several pages. The chips name the paper and the table behind each number.

  • One component carries most of the gain. Removing the frozen encoder raises Method C's Benchmark Q error from 19.6 to 31.4 Method C Tab.5, and Method D loses 6.0 of its 8.8-point gain over Method B without it Method D Tab.4. The other additions change the error by less than 1.
  • Most priors stay frozen. Methods C and D keep Encoder W frozen and train only a small head (38 M and 120 M parameters); Method D's fine-tuning ablation is worse on both benchmarks Method D Tab.6.
  • Released code differs from the paper. Method B's released code skips the refinement stage the paper describes, and Method C's loss weights differ from the paper's table train.py:88.
  • The benchmark changes the ranking. On Method E's data, Method A beats both feed-forward methods Method E Tab.4, the opposite of their own papers.
